# Getting started

[← Wiki home](https://github.com/zaera/Pika_note/wiki/Home)

## A small device. A familiar companion.
Pika records your thoughts with a physical button. Pika Companion on Android keeps your library, connects to online services and lets you continue Ask without the device.

## What you need
- A supported Pika ESP32-S3 e-paper device with a working microSD card and the matching V8 firmware.
- Pika Companion for Android 8 or later. Enable Bluetooth and grant the permissions Android requests. Older Android versions may also require Location for Bluetooth discovery.
- Your own OpenAI API key and internet on the phone for recognition, AI and voice responses. Provider usage may incur charges; a ChatGPT subscription is separate.

## First pairing
1. Wake Pika with the lower button and open Companion. Choose Get started, then Find Pika.
2. Select your Pika and confirm the pairing code shown on its screen. The short PN serial identifies the device; it is not the pairing code.
3. Complete setup: OpenAI key is required, then choose a voice. Male · Cedar is the default.
4. Optionally connect Calendar and Gmail, choose a theme and set a four-digit application PIN. Light is the default theme.
5. If the app closes before setup finishes, open it again. The saved pairing is kept and setup resumes. A missing API key returns you to the required key step.

## Your first memory
Hold the upper button, speak, then release. This saves a memory, even if you mention work or appointments. Ask is where you request tasks and events. Recordings shorter than half a second are discarded as accidental presses.

## Your first Ask
Hold the lower button, speak, then release. Try: “What memories do I have?” or “Create a task to call Alex tomorrow by 2 pm.” You can also type or hold the microphone in the app's Ask tab. Use Russian or English naturally.

## Keep the companion available
Allow Bluetooth and background operation for Companion in Android settings. If your phone aggressively limits background apps, allow unrestricted battery use for Companion. The device advertises while awake; reconnection and queued transfers resume when the phone is available. Pika sleeps to save battery, so an offline phone edit may wait until its next wake.
