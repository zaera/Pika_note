## Today and navigation
Today shows active memories, open tasks and events for today / upcoming events. Completed tasks are not counted as open. Mail appears when configured. Tap the counters on the app illustration to open their sections. Battery percentage, voltage and SD free / total space appear in the top status panel.

Use the bottom icons for Today, Memories, Tasks, Events, Ask and Config. Swipe horizontally to move between main sections; reverse an unfinished swipe to cancel it. Archive and Calendar are available in Config. The calendar icon beside a list heading opens Calendar. Android Back closes a dialog first; otherwise it returns to Today.

## Physical controls
Default actions on Today:
- Upper single: tasks. Upper double: daily briefing. Upper hold: record memory. Upper triple: no action.
- Lower single: events. Lower double: weekly briefing. Lower hold: Ask. Lower triple: clock.
- Hold both buttons to request a companion reconnection. This is not a local Wi-Fi web-server command in V8.

Change single, double, triple and hold assignments in Config в†’ Home button actions. Record and Ask are offered on hold slots. Inside an active screen, that screen's controls take over; Today combinations resume after returning home. The month calendar and weather close with either button. Lower / Back stops Mail narration and leaves Mail. Clock stays awake until exited; the other extra screens return to Today before timed sleep.

## Memories
Memories contain your original recording and transcript. Edit the transcript to correct recognition; it stays a memory. In the app, Add memory offers a pencil for text or a microphone for hold-to-record. Voice notes are kept for later processing if internet is unavailable. Manually editing an untranscribed note prevents automatic recognition from overwriting your text.

Copy copies the transcript. Share opens Android sharing with the transcript and WAV when available. TXT and WAV downloads are also offered in the editor. Forget archives a memory and removes its WAV; restoring it brings back text only. A complete earlier backup may still contain the old recording.

## Tasks, events and archives
Tasks support priority and an optional deadline. No deadline means the task stays open until you complete or archive it. Events support a start/end, all-day dates and multi-day spans. Past events remain on their calendar dates and appear in Archive; they are not automatically erased.

Archive search matches titles, full text and related fields across dates. Archived task and memory lists show the newest archive changes first, with 24 items per page. Archive's trash action permanently deletes all archived data after confirmation. Undo and restore depend on retained recovery copies; keep a full backup before destructive cleanup.

## Sleep and offline operation
The inverted Today screen indicates sleep; waking returns to the light device screen. On the app, the sleeping illustration is dimmed. The activity LED, when enabled, indicates work or communication; the red charging indicator is independent.

Without the phone, Pika still records memories and queues them. With the phone but no internet, recordings wait for recognition. Offline Ask recordings can be transferred later: actionable requests are handled in capture order; ordinary conversational questions are skipped. Weather and Mail keep cached information after a failed refresh. Ask and live service screens may show No connection or Try again when their dependencies are unavailable.
